## Open your library

Use the dashboard button in Darb's popup. Your library remains accessible when you are not on a video page.

**Today** and **Continue** help you return to learning. **Library** brings courses, playlists and notes together. Open an item to inspect its details.

## Find material

Search titles, tags and note text. Combine folder and material filters, then choose a list, table or card view. Folder filters include nested folders. Your view and filter preferences are stored locally.

## Organize several items

Select items with their checkboxes. Choose a destination and press **Move** to apply it. Picking an option alone does not complete the move. You can also replace tags for the selected material.
