## Is Darb free?

Darb is free and open source under the MIT license. Your chosen AI service can have its own subscription or usage costs.

## Do I need an account or AI app?

No Darb account is required. Courses and ordinary study features work without MCP or an AI connection.

## Where is my progress saved?

In your browser profile's local extension storage. Different profiles and devices do not automatically share a library. Removing the extension or resetting its data can erase it.

## Does Darb download videos?

No. Videos play on their original platform. Darb tracks and controls the active learning session there.

## Does it support X and Facebook?

Newer development builds include experimental support for individual recorded X posts and dedicated Facebook video pages. Access is optional. Feeds, live streams, Reels, Stories and ambiguous multi-video pages are unsupported. Do not expect these features in the original 0.1.0 release.

## Does a Darb playlist create a YouTube playlist?

No. A Darb playlist is stored locally in your learning workspace.

## Can my assistant read my notes?

Current MCP responses and assistant exports exclude notes, folders, tags and graph positions. Only explicitly shared learning material and permitted operations are exposed.

## Does local MCP mean AI processing stays on my computer?

No. The bridge communicates locally, but your AI app may send the data it receives to its model provider. See [privacy](/docs/privacy/).

## Is the documentation website an MCP server?

No. It hosts guides. The MCP companion runs locally and connects to the selected browser profile.

## How do I install Darb on Chrome?

Install from the [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/darb/abfplmdaigobogelhdalmbcmlhdocloj). It also supports Brave and Edge. Store installations update automatically; no ZIP or Developer mode is needed.

## Will an unpacked extension update itself?

No. [Update the existing local installation](/docs/install/#update-a-local-installation) when a new release is available.
