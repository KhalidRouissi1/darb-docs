Darb · درب turns a long YouTube video into sessions that fit your day. Choose a daily study time, make a start, and return to where you stopped.

<Tutorial id="overview" />

## Start with one video

1. [Install Darb](/docs/install/) and pin it to your browser toolbar.
2. Open a recorded YouTube video you want to study.
3. Click Darb, choose your study duration and create a course.
4. Start learning. Darb tracks your place and pauses at the session boundary.

You do not need a Darb account, an AI subscription or terminal commands to use ordinary course features.

## Give your learning a home

The newer workspace brings together courses, playlists, folders, tags and video notes. Use the dashboard to find your next lesson, or explore relationships in the graph.

<ReleaseNotice />

## Connect an assistant when it helps

The optional [AI connection](/docs/ai/) lets Codex or Claude Code read the courses you explicitly share. Ask what to study next or request a plan for the time you have. Ordinary Darb use works without it.

## What stays on your computer

Courses, progress and notes live in your browser profile. Darb does not upload them to the developer. If you share course data with an AI app, that app may send it to its model provider. [Read the privacy guide](/docs/privacy/).
