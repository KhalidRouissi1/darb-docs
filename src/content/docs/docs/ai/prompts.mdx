---
title: "Example prompts"
description: "Useful prompts for shared courses, study plans and reviewed playlist proposals."
---

Start with a read-only request. The assistant can work only with material you explicitly shared.

## Verify your connection

```text
Use Darb to list my shared courses. Tell me which are in progress.
```

## Find your next session

```text
Use Darb to show my next session for one of my shared courses.
Tell me where I will resume and how much of that session remains.
```

If there is more than one course, choose a returned title or ID. Do not assume the assistant can see unshared material.

## Plan a short study block

```text
I have 30 minutes. Use my shared Darb courses to preview a study plan.
Don't open a video yet. Explain if the plan ends partway through a session.
```

This plans time from course metadata and saved progress. It does not summarize video transcripts or check your understanding.

## Propose a playlist from your selection

Live access only: enable selection drafting, then enter YouTube selection mode and pick videos first.

```text
Use the YouTube videos I selected in Darb to preview a learning playlist.
Show the order and ask me before requesting its save.
```

Review the proposed save in Darb → Assistant. An expired or changed selection may require a fresh preview.

## Request playback

Enable playback requests separately, then ask:

```text
Use Darb to request opening my next course session.
```

Approve or reject the request in Darb. The assistant does not immediately control playback and cannot mark sessions completed.
