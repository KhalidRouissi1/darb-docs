A Darb playlist is a local learning path. It does not create a playlist in your YouTube account.

<Tutorial id="playlists" />

## Select videos

1. Open YouTube's home, search results or a channel with video cards.
2. Click Darb and choose **Select videos on YouTube**.
3. In selection mode, click a video card to select or deselect it. The whole card is a selection target, including the thumbnail.
4. Review your selection and save it as a Darb playlist.
5. Open **Playlists** to choose and study your saved path.

Exit selection mode when you want normal video links to open again. Selection alone does not start a course.

## Rename or edit

In Playlists, choose **Rename / edit playlist** beside the relevant playlist. You can also select the playlist in Library and edit its title in the inspector.

## Completion and the next video

The in-video playlist controls show your position, completed count and next video's title. **Next video** continues the path in the learning tab.

When a tracked video reaches its natural end, Darb records video completion and advances to the next video. The final video should leave the playlist marked complete. A study session ending partway through a long video is not the end of that video.

**Check it:** finish a short test video naturally and confirm the completed count increases before the next one starts. [Report a playback issue](/docs/troubleshooting/) if it doesn't.

## Delete a playlist

Deleting a playlist removes the grouping. Individual courses and their saved checkpoints remain in your library.
